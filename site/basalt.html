Basalt: live dual filter

Basalt is a free, open-source effect plugin. Stereo dual filter for live use.

Version 1.2.0 comes with 31 factory presets and a manual. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux as VST3, AU, CLAP, with VST2 on Windows and Linux. It costs nothing and the source is public under the MIT licence.
