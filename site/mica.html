Mica: equalizer

Mica is a free, open-source effect plugin. 24-band stereo equalizer with dynamic and spectral EQ, EQ Match and three phase modes.

Version 2.1.0 comes with 39 factory presets and a manual. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux as VST3, AU, CLAP, with VST2 on Windows and Linux. It costs nothing and the source is public under the MIT licence.
