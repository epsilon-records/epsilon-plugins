Obsidian: polysynth

Obsidian is a free, open-source instrument plugin. Polyphonic four-oscillator, four-filter synthesizer with effects and an arpeggiator, up to 64 voices.

Version 3.1.0 comes with 60 factory presets and a manual. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux as VST3, AU, CLAP, with VST2 on Windows and Linux. It costs nothing and the source is public under the MIT licence.
