Gneiss: compressor

Gneiss is a free, open-source effect plugin. Stereo compressor with 14 styles, lookahead, oversampling, a sidechain EQ and loudness metering.

Version 2.1.0 comes with 46 factory presets and a manual. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux as VST3, AU, CLAP, with VST2 on Windows and Linux. It costs nothing and the source is public under the MIT licence.
